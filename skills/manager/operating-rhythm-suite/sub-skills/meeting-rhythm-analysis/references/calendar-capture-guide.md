# Calendar Capture Guide

Give the manager these options when no calendar is connected. Three months of data is
best because it shows quarterly reviews; any data is better than none.

## Option 1: Full export (most accurate)

**Microsoft Outlook (desktop)**
1. File, then Open & Export, then Import/Export.
2. Choose "Export to a file", then CSV.
3. Select the Calendar folder.
4. Set the date range to the last 3 months.
5. Save as calendar-export.csv and upload it.

**Google Calendar**
1. Settings (gear icon), then Settings.
2. Import & export in the left menu, then Export.
3. Unzip the downloaded file and upload the .ics file for your own calendar.

## Option 2: Strategic screenshots (fastest)

Take 4 to 5 week-view screenshots:
1. This week (required).
2. A week from last month (required).
3. The week of your last quarterly review, if there was one.
4. A heavy meeting week (optional).
5. A light meeting week (optional).

Good screenshots use week view, show 7 AM to 7 PM, show meeting titles clearly and
cover Monday to Friday at least. Blur customer names if needed. Month view and phone
screenshots cut off titles.

## Option 3: Hybrid

Export one month, plus a screenshot of the last quarterly review week.

## Quick choice

- Under 2 minutes, or low comfort with exports: screenshots.
- 2 to 4 minutes: one-month export plus the quarterly review screenshot.
- 4 minutes or more: full 3-month export.

## How the report changes

With a full export, figures are measured across the period. With screenshots, every
figure says "based on N sample weeks", rhythms outside the captured weeks are NOT
VISIBLE, and the report asks for the missing week rather than guessing.
