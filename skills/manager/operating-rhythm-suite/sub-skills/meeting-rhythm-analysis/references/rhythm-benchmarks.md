# Rhythm Benchmarks

The expected rhythms this skill checks a calendar against. Core rhythms come from the
Dana operating rhythm (dana-sales-management-brief-v2.md section VI,
methodology/60-min-1on1-structure.md, methodology/bi-monthly-forecast-cadence.md).
When manager.cadence sets a frequency, the manager's setting replaces the default.

## Core rhythms

| Rhythm | Benchmark | Calendar signals | INCONSISTENT when | Minimum data to judge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly team meeting | 45-60 min, weekly. Results and metrics, forecast updates, skill of the week, deal spotlight, coordination. Not for strategy or deep coaching. | Recurring event with most or all direct reports; "team meeting", "kickoff", "weekly". | Over 90 min; missing in more than 1 week in 4; several overlapping team meetings with the same attendees. | 2 weeks |
| Weekly 1:1 | 60 min per rep, weekly. Never cancelled, only rescheduled; calendared a quarter ahead. | Manager plus one direct report; rep name with "1:1", "1on1", "one on one", "check-in". | Under 45 min; every other week or less often; any rep with no match; frequent cancellations visible in the series. | 2 weeks |
| Team focus session | 60 min, every other week, alternating: deep-dive deal review (2 deals) one week, skill workshop the next. | "Deal review", "deep dive", "skill workshop", "training", "role play" with the team. | Only one of the two formats ever appears; weekly full-pipeline review used instead. | 4 weeks |
| Forecast review | Bi-monthly: start-of-month comprehensive review (90 min) and mid-month checkpoint (45 min). | "Forecast", "commit", "BOM", "mid-month". | Weekly forecast or pipeline call; no start-of-month review; no mid-month checkpoint. | 6 weeks |
| Quarterly business review | Half day, quarterly: performance, market and competition, strategy adjustment, team development, next-quarter planning. | "QBR", "quarterly", "planning", "offsite", 3 or more hours, team attendees. | Under 3 hours; no team involvement; leader presentation only. | 13 weeks or a screenshot of the QBR week |

## Optional rhythms (from the source bot)

Report these only when the manager says they run them or asks about them.

| Rhythm | Benchmark | INCONSISTENT when |
|---|---|---|
| Daily team huddle | 15-30 min, each person shares one win, the day's top priority and any roadblock. Not a problem-solving session. | Over 45 min, or fewer than 3 days a week. |
| Separate monthly development review | 45-60 min per rep, monthly, distinct from the weekly 1:1. | Not applicable as a gap in the Dana model, where career development sits inside every 1:1 and the monthly topic rotation. Report presence only. |

## Status rules

- FOUND needs the rhythm across most of the period at roughly the benchmark length.
- MISSING needs at least the minimum data in the table; otherwise NOT VISIBLE.
- With screenshots, judge only what the captured weeks show, and say so.

## What not to claim

The calendar shows that a meeting was scheduled, its length and who was invited. It
does not show attendance, the agenda that was followed or the quality of the
conversation. Do not attach improvement percentages to any rhythm unless a bundled
methodology file states the figure with its source.
